This is a deterministic educational simulation of a 2 × 2 fixed-effects factorial experiment with a shared sample SD. The F-tests are equal-weight, cell-mean-contrast tests with error degrees of freedom N − 4. They coincide with standard balanced two-way ANOVA tests when all four cell sizes are equal. For unequal cell sizes, these are adjusted tests of equally weighted marginal-mean contrasts, not sequential ANOVA sums of squares; interpret main effects alongside the interaction.
